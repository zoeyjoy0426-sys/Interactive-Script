02 / About

A creative, research-led approach

I work at the intersection of storytelling, visual culture and emerging media. My interests include adaptation across artistic forms, interactive narrative, and the ways media shape audience engagement.

My academic training in drama, film and television literature, followed by an MSc in Intermediality, informs a practice that combines critical thinking with hands-on digital experimentation.

I am interested in opportunities involving creative content, digital storytelling, cultural media, editorial work and cross-media projects.
