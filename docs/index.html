01 · Featured project

Dot by Dot

From stage to interactive page. An adaptation of Sunday in the Park with George into an interactive digital graphic script made with Twine.

Through still images, dialogue and branching navigation, the project explores how theatrical narrative, character relationships and audience engagement can be reconfigured across media.

Developed as an MSc practice-based project at the University of Edinburgh, the work received 73% (Distinction).

02 · Creative process

Making stories visible

The visual world of Dot by Dot was developed through character and costume studies, scene composition, and digital image-making. It draws on the atmosphere of nineteenth-century painting while reimagining the spatial language of performance.

I / ADAPTATION

From performance to page

Reworking stage space, dialogue and dramatic sequence for a navigable digital format.

II / IMAGE

Visual worldbuilding

Developing characters, costumes and painterly scenes as parts of a coherent narrative environment.

III / INTERACTION

Choice and spectatorship

Designing moments of exploration within a deliberately structured emotional story.
